About the lot

Quality. Trust. Drive Sherway.

Every vehicle is hand-picked, inspected and sold with transparency.

We're a small, independent lot in Concord, Ontario — not a big-box dealership. That means every car that comes through the gate gets looked at closely before it goes up for sale, and every price is the price: no hidden fees, no last-minute add-ons, no pressure.

CARFAX reports are available on request for every vehicle we sell, financing can be arranged, and safety certification is handled before delivery. If you want to know what's coming in next, or you're just not sure what you're looking for yet, call the lot — we'll talk it through with you.
